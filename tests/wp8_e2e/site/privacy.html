Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rihla Delivery collects and uses personal data when you use our app and website.

Data we collect

We collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery addresses, order history, device identifiers and, with your permission, your location while you use the app.

Why we use it

We use your data to deliver orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud and, if you agree, send you offers. You can withdraw your consent to marketing messages at any time from the app settings.

Who we share it with

We share the delivery address and phone number with the rider who delivers your order. We do not sell your personal data. Service providers that host our systems process data only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for five years to meet accounting obligations, and are then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data by contacting our data protection officer through the Help section of the app. We answer requests within 30 days.
